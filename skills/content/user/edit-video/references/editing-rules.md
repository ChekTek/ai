# Editing Rules

A-roll: the talking-head footage. B-roll: downloaded gameplay, trailers, and
website screenshots.

## Always

- Never generate video, image, or audio assets. Everything comes from the
  sources, downloads, or screenshots.
- No color grading. No intro, outro, or end-screen templates.
- The first 30 seconds matter most. Edit them first and review them again at
  the end. The rest can be done with less time.

## A-roll cuts

- Cut pauses longer than 0.6 s down to 0.25 s. Leave shorter breaths alone.
- Remove false starts and retakes. A retake is the same sentence or phrase said
  again within about 30 s. Keep the last take unless the speaker says
  otherwise on camera.
- Cut only in silence, at least 3 frames clear of the neighboring word
  boundaries in the transcript. Never cut inside a word.
- Follow the order in the brief when one is given.

## Retention

- Cut to B-roll or a zoom every 5 to 10 s in the beginning of the video (first minute).
- If A-roll stays on screen longer than 10 s, add a slow zoom: 100 to 108 %
  across the clip, eased in and out.
- If a thought runs longer than 10 s, prefer a longer B-roll section over
  A-roll.
- Punch-in on a cut: 115 to 120 %. Return to 100 % on a later cut, never
  mid-clip.

## B-roll

- Show what is being discussed: a menu feature shows that menu, a boss shows
  that fight.
- A single point (a stat, a price, a patch note, a post) gets a website
  screenshot rather than more gameplay.
- Quotes from people or publications go on a Quote Card over blurred B-roll
  for as long as they are read.
- Community (unofficial) footage carries a Lower Third Credit with the
  channel's `@handle` for the whole clip. Official publisher, platform, and
  developer channels need no credit. See `fetch-b-roll.md`.
- B-roll is full-frame on V2. It covers the A-roll picture; A-roll audio keeps
  playing.

## Audio (provisional levels; tune later)

- A-roll dialogue: -14 LUFS integrated (ITU-R BS.1770-4), peaks at or below
  -1 dBTP.
- B-roll audio: -28 dB relative to dialogue, or muted if it adds nothing.
- Music, only when supplied: -30 dB under dialogue, ducked a further 6 dB while
  speaking. Otherwise leave the A-roll without music.
- Check the mix at the end: dialogue is always clearly on top.
